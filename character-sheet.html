Effective SP uses the higher of current worn-armor SP or applicable cyberware armor. Ablation remains tracked in Current Worn SP and is never automatically restored. Armor penalties remain manually entered because they depend on the worn armor combination.
Shield
No Shield
A Shield isn't SP — it doesn't reduce damage. While equipped and raised, the wielder is in Cover; if attacked by a target they can see, they may interpose the Shield, which takes the Attack's full damage in place of the wearer. When Shield HP reaches 0 it's destroyed: it stops providing Cover but stays equipped until replaced. Core Rulebook p. 170.
